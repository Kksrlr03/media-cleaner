Will my image or video lose quality?

PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF are cleaned at the file-container level. Their encoded image data stays unchanged. Videos use stream copy, so no video frames or audio are re-encoded. Color, orientation, HDR and animation information needed for correct playback are preserved. Unsupported files fail safely instead of being downgraded.

What gets removed—and what stays?

Descriptive metadata such as GPS, camera and software fields, timestamps, comments, XMP and generation prompts is removed where present. Necessary rendering data such as color profiles and orientation may remain. Video subtitles, chapters, cover art and non-media attachments are excluded. Codec-level data and watermarks inside the media itself may remain. The report tells you what was checked.

Where do my files go?

Nowhere. Files are processed locally in browser memory. There are no media uploads, accounts, analytics or media databases. Video tools download on first use. Save uses your browser's file picker when supported, or its normal downloads. Delete clears in-tab references and working files; it cannot erase your original or previously downloaded copies.

Does this remove AI labels or visible watermarks?

No. Metadata cleaning does not guarantee that Instagram or an AI detector will classify a file differently. It does not remove visible or pixel-embedded watermarks.
